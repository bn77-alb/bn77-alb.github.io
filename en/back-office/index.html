Back-office services

Back office: operational support for your company

SION Consulting supports small and medium-sized companies in Germany from our office. We take over recurring administrative tasks so your team can focus on the core business.

Request back-office support

What we do for you

Recruitment support

Screening applicant files, coordinating interviews and communicating with candidates.

Logistics administration

Maintaining and following up route documents, delivery notes and order data.

Document processing

Contracts, letters and standard documents based on your templates – clean and on time.

Data entry

Entering and maintaining data in your lists and systems.

Email administration

Sorting your inbox, answering standard requests, forwarding what matters to you.

Customer communication

Friendly, reliable communication with your customers – in German or English.

Appointment coordination

Scheduling, confirming and maintaining appointments in your calendar.

Driver & document management

Driver files, licence and driver card checks, certificates and deadlines under control.

General administration

Flexible help with recurring office tasks – scope as agreed.

Confidentiality and data protection
