Recruitment into direct employment

SION Consulting is a recruitment agency for companies in Germany looking for drivers, warehouse, cleaning, construction, kitchen, production and seasonal staff. We handle the search, check experience and documents in advance and only introduce candidates who fit your position.

Unlike temporary staffing, the employees sign their employment contract directly with your company. After the interview you decide yourself whom to hire – and you only pay once the cooperation works.

For candidates from abroad we also support the administrative steps: documents for the Federal Employment Agency, the visa procedure, arrival and the first days in Germany.

Frequently asked questions about recruitment

What does recruitment cost?

You pay a one-off placement fee per employee, with no deposit. The invoice only comes after 30 working days; if the employee leaves before then, you pay nothing. The amount depends on the position and the number of employees – you receive an individual offer in advance.

How long does it take until an employee can start?

Candidates who already live in Germany are often available at short notice. For candidates from abroad the duration depends on the procedure – under the Western Balkans regulation often several months.

Is this temporary agency work?

No. All employees are employed directly by you. This is not temporary agency work.

Which positions do you recruit for?

Truck drivers with a C/CE licence, parcel delivery drivers, warehouse and logistics staff, cleaning staff, construction workers and helpers (including civil engineering), kitchen helpers and cooks, production and assembly helpers and seasonal workers.

In which regions do you work?

We recruit for companies across Germany. Coordination takes place by email, video and phone – in German or English.

What do we need to contribute as an employer?

A clear job description, an interview, an employment contract on terms customary in Germany and, for candidates from abroad, the information the authorities need. We help with the forms.
