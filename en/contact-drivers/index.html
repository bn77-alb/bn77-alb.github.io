2. Common questions before you contact us

Does it cost me anything?
No. The employer pays us.
How long until I start?
Usually a few weeks to a few months, depending on the embassy.
Where do I apply for the visa?
Usually at the German embassy in your country of residence.
Which documents do I need?
Passport, residence permit, EU driving licence C/CE with Code 95, driver card, CV.
Do I need to speak German?
Not for every job.
I have already applied – when will you contact me?
Within 1–2 working days by email. Please also check your spam folder.

4. What happens after your message

  1. You immediately receive a confirmation by email.
  2. We check your details within 1–2 working days.
  3. If your profile fits, we send you a link for a short call with us.
  4. Then we introduce you to suitable employers.
5. Beware of fraud

We never ask drivers for money – not for placement, visa or documents. We only write from addresses ending in @sionconsulting.de. If someone asks you for money in our name, write to us: bewerbung@sionconsulting.de
