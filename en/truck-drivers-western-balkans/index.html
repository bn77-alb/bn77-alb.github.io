Western Balkans regulation: truck drivers from the Western Balkans

Drivers from Albania, Kosovo, North Macedonia, Serbia, Montenegro and Bosnia and Herzegovina can work in Germany under the Western Balkans regulation. We find suitable CE drivers and support the administrative steps – up to the first working day.

Explained simply

What is the Western Balkans regulation?

The Western Balkans regulation (Section 26(2) of the German Employment Ordinance) allows citizens of Albania, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Kosovo, Montenegro, North Macedonia and Serbia to work in Germany – even without a recognised vocational qualification.

A concrete job offer is required. The Federal Employment Agency must approve it and checks, among other things, the working conditions. Temporary agency work is excluded – the driver is employed directly by you.

The number of approvals per year is limited (currently up to 50,000). A reduction has been announced. We keep you informed about the current situation.

Driving licence

Conversion without a test

Licences from Albania, Kosovo, North Macedonia, Serbia and Montenegro – including classes C and CE – can be converted in Germany without a theory or practical test (Annex 11 of the German Driving Licence Regulation).

For licences from Bosnia and Herzegovina, this does not apply to classes C and CE.

The driver also needs the professional driver qualification (Code 95 or driver qualification card). We clarify the steps needed in each case before hiring.

Frequently asked questions about the Western Balkans regulation

Which countries does the Western Balkans regulation cover?

Citizens of Albania, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Kosovo, Montenegro, North Macedonia and Serbia.

Does the driver need a recognised qualification?

No. The Western Balkans regulation does not require a recognised vocational qualification. To drive, however, he needs a C/CE licence and the professional driver qualification.

Can the driver be employed through a temporary agency?

No. Temporary agency work is excluded. The driver is employed directly by you.

How long does it take?

It depends on the quota, visa appointments and the individual case – often several months. We check each case individually; the decision is made by the authorities.
