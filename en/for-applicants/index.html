Jobs in Germany – apply for free

We place truck drivers and workers for warehouses, construction, cleaning and seasonal work directly with employers in Germany. Please check first if we can place you.

✓ Who we can place

  1. Truck drivers from non-EU countries who already live and work in an EU countryMain focus with a residence permit there – ideally with an EU licence C/CE and Code 95.
    Information for drivers in the EU
  2. Citizens of the Western Balkans Albania, Kosovo, North Macedonia, Serbia, Montenegro, Bosnia and Herzegovina.
  3. EU and EEA citizens
  4. People who already live in Germany and are allowed to work here also drivers who want to change employer.

What you need

  • A residence permit in an EU country or citizenship from the groups above
  • A licence for the job (truck: C/CE and Code 95)
  • At least 1 year of work experience
  • A clean criminal record

What we do for you

  • A suitable job with an employer in Germany – direct employment, no temporary agency
  • Help with documents and appointments
  • Help to find an apartment or a shared flat
  • Contact person in German and English

How your application works

  1. Fill in the form

    First you check if we can place you. Then you add your details.

  2. Interview

    We get to know you and check your documents.

  3. Meet the employer

    The employer gets to know you – often by video.

  4. Documents & start

    We help with the paperwork up to your first working day.

We check your case individually; the decision is made by the German authorities. We help with administrative steps. We do not give legal advice.

Questions from applicants

From which countries can I apply?

We place: 1. truck drivers from non-EU countries who already live and work in an EU country with a residence permit; 2. citizens of Albania, Kosovo, North Macedonia, Serbia, Montenegro and Bosnia and Herzegovina; 3. EU and EEA citizens; 4. people who already live in Germany and are allowed to work here. If you do not live in the EU and have no residence permit in an EU country, you cannot apply at the moment.

Do I need to speak German?

It depends on the job and the employer. It is not needed for every job, but German always helps.

Do I have to pay?

No. We never ask applicants for money. The employers pay for our service.

Which documents do I need?

For the application, the details in the form are enough. We ask for your CV, licence and residence permit in the next step.
