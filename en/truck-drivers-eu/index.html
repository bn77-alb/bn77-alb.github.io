For truck drivers who live in the EU

Already driving in the EU? Move to a truck job in Germany.

This is for you if you …

  • live and work legally in an EU country (e.g. Poland, Lithuania, Netherlands, Czech Republic) with a residence permit – any nationality
  • have a driving licence C/CE and Code 95
  • want better pay, fixed routes and to live in Germany
Apply in 1 minute

Free for drivers. We never take money from drivers.

Why Germany, why us

What you get as a truck driver in Germany

Permanent contract

A permanent contract directly with the employer. No temporary agency.

German pay

German wages, daily allowances (Spesen) and German social security.

Fixed routes

Regional work – home every day is possible – or long-haul.

Apartment or shared flat

We help you find an apartment or a room in a shared flat.

Free for drivers

You pay nothing. The employer pays for our service.

How it works

5 steps to your truck job in Germany

  1. Apply

    The form takes 1 minute.

  2. We check

    Your residence permit, licence and Code 95.

  3. Video interview

    You talk to the employer by video.

  4. Paperwork

    We help with the papers for the German work and residence permit.

  5. Start in Germany

    You start with your new employer.

How long does it take? It depends on your case – often a few weeks to a few months. We check your case individually; the decision is made by the German authorities.

What you need

  • A valid residence permit in an EU country (e.g. karta pobytu, leidimas gyventi, verblijfsvergunning …)
  • Driving licence C/CE (EU licence preferred) with Code 95
  • At least 1 year of experience as a truck driver
  • A clean criminal record
  • Basic German is a plus. It is not needed for every job.
Free for drivers

We never ask applicants for money. If someone asks you for money in our name, please tell us.

We help with administrative steps. We do not give legal advice. The competent authorities make the binding decision.

Questions from truck drivers in the EU

I have a residence permit in Poland or Lithuania. Can I work in Germany?

Often yes, but it depends on your permit and nationality. We check your documents and explain the next steps; the decision is made by the German authorities.

Can I work in Germany automatically with my residence permit from another EU country?

No, usually not. A residence permit from Poland, Lithuania or another EU country normally does not allow you to work in Germany. For work here you usually need your own permit from the German authorities. We help with the papers; the decision is made by the German authorities.

Is my EU driving licence valid in Germany?

Yes, an EU licence and Code 95 are valid in Germany.

Do I have to pay?

No. We never take money from drivers.

Do I need German?

Not for every job. It helps.
