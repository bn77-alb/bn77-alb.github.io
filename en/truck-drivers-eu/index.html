How you get to Germany

  1. Apply

    Form, about 1 minute.

  2. Check

    We check your residence permit, licence and Code 95.

  3. Video interview

    You talk to the employer.

  4. Employment contract

    You get a concrete job offer.

  5. Approval of the employment agency

    The employer applies for it under Section 24a BeschV. The agency checks that pay and conditions are fair.

  6. Visa

    You apply at the German embassy in your country of residence (residence permit under Section 19c(1) AufenthG). The accelerated skilled worker procedure is often possible.

  7. Start in Germany

    Registration, first working day.

How long does it take? Usually a few weeks to a few months, depending on the embassy. The authorities make the decision.

Documents you need

Frequently asked questions

I have a karta pobytu in Poland. Can I work in Germany as a truck driver?

Yes, if you have an EU driving licence C/CE with Code 95 and a job offer from Germany. The employer gets the approval of the Federal Employment Agency, you apply for the visa. We prepare the documents with you.

Can I simply start working in Germany with my Polish or Lithuanian residence permit?

No. For a permanent job in Germany you need a German residence permit. For professional drivers, however, the procedure has its own rules (Section 24a BeschV) and is very doable.

Is my licence with Code 95 valid in Germany?

An EU driving licence is valid in Germany, and Code 95 from an EU country is recognised. You then do the further training every 5 years in Germany.

Do I need to speak German?

Not for every job. Many employers accept English or basic German. German is a plus.

How long does it take?

Usually a few weeks to a few months, depending on the embassy in your country of residence.

What does it cost me?

Nothing. The employer pays us.

Can my family come with me?

Generally yes, through family reunification. The embassy checks the requirements.

We help with administrative steps; we do not give legal advice. The competent authorities make the binding decision.
